Good to know

A few common questions.

Still wondering if this is for you? Here are a few helpful answers.

Do I need experience to join?

Not at all. Beginners are welcome, and there are plenty of ways to learn alongside other students.

What can I work on as a member?

You can join group projects, explore an area of technology, share your skills, or bring an idea of your own.

How much time do I need to commit?

Start with what fits your schedule. You can take part in the activities that work for you and adjust as you go.
