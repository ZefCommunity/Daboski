We learn to build by building together.

We are a community of students who share one goal: getting better at technology by making real things, reviewing each other's work, and helping each other grow.

Join the community

Why we exist

Learning to code from tutorials only goes so far. The skills that matter most, like working with a team, reading someone else's code, and shipping something people can use, only come from doing it.

Our community gives students a place to practise those skills together. Beginners are welcome, and nobody is expected to know everything. We ask questions, share what we learn, and review each other's work with kindness.

What we do

Learn

Beginner-friendly courses and study sessions that cover HTML, CSS, Git, and more.

Build

Hands-on projects where members design, code, and launch real websites and apps.

Connect

Events, talks, and meetups where students meet each other and people working in tech.

Share

A blog and project showcase where members write about what they have learned and made.

How we work

We use the same workflow professional development teams use, so every member leaves with experience that carries over to a real job.

  1. Clone the shared repository to your computer.
  2. Branch off main so your work stays separate.
  3. Build your page with HTML and CSS.
  4. Commit and push your changes with a clear message.
  5. Open a pull request and wait for review before it joins main.

What we value

Learning in the open
Mistakes are part of the process. We share them so everyone learns.
Respectful feedback
We review the work, not the person, and we say what is good as well as what to fix.
Everyone contributes
Every member builds something real, whatever their experience level.
Finishing things
We ship small, working pieces instead of leaving big ideas unfinished.
