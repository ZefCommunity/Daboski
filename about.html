About us

We learn to build by building together.

We are a community of students who want to get better at technology by making real things, reviewing each other's work, and helping each other grow.

Join the community Ask a question

Who it's for

Anyone who wants to learn by doing, at any level.

  • Complete beginners
  • Future developers
  • Designers
  • Curious students

Why we exist

Learning to code from tutorials only goes so far. The skills that matter most, like working with a team, reading someone else's code, and shipping something people can use, only come from doing it.

Our community gives students a place to practise those skills together. Beginners are welcome, and nobody is expected to know everything. We ask questions, share what we learn, and review each other's work with kindness.

How we work

We use the same workflow professional development teams use, so every member leaves with experience that carries over to a real job.

  1. Clone

    Copy the shared repository to your computer.

  2. Branch

    Create your own branch so your work stays separate from main.

  3. Build

    Make your page with HTML and CSS.

  4. Commit and push

    Save your changes with a clear message and push your branch to GitHub.

  5. Open a pull request

    Ask for a review. Your work joins main once it is approved.
