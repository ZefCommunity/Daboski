Small steps, real progress

From first idea to first release

Keep the first version simple. Learn as you go, share your progress, and invite others to contribute.

  1. Choose a problem

    Find something you or your community would like to make easier, more useful, or more fun.

  2. Plan a small first version

    Write down who it is for and the one thing your first prototype should help them do.

  3. Build, test, and share

    Make a simple prototype, ask for feedback, and improve it with your collaborators.

Your turn

Have an idea of your own?

Bring a question, a rough sketch, or a skill you want to practice. A project can start small and grow with the people who join it.

Build in the open

Use a branch for your work, make clear commits, and open a pull request so teammates can review and contribute.
